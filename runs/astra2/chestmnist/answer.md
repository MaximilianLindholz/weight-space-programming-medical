I would use **fixed anatomical contrast and texture proxies**, not pretend these weights are learned or validated.

**Filters:** 0 measures Gaussian-smoothed brightness, with a bias keeping it linear; 1 detects unusually bright regions; 2 detects dark regions; 3 and 4 detect local bright and dark peaks; 5/6 detect opposite horizontal brightness transitions; 7/8 detect opposite vertical transitions; 9 detects horizontal bright ridges. Most brightness readouts subtract another chest region to reduce sensitivity to overall exposure. Outer image borders are excluded.
